Mealvori: AI Calorie Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026.

Information used by the app

Mealvori lets you enter information such as sex, date of birth, height, weight, target weight, activity level, food and exercise entries, water intake, and reminder and language preferences. It uses these details to provide nutrition targets and track your activity and water intake. These records are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The current build does not offer user accounts or synchronize these records to a developer-operated database.

Camera and photos

The app requests camera access for its food-scanning interface when the feature is used. The current AI recognition service is not configured. If a food image is saved in the app, it is stored in the app's internal storage. You can manage camera permission in your device settings.

Diagnostics and service providers

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and diagnostic information to help identify and fix problems. This may include crash traces, app version, device and operating system information, and installation identifiers. Google processes this data as a service provider. See Firebase privacy information for information about Firebase's data practices.

The app contains Google Mobile Ads SDK components, but ad requests and display are disabled in this test build. If advertising or additional services are enabled in a future version, we will update this policy to describe their data practices.

Backup, retention and deletion

App data is kept on your device until you remove it, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Depending on device settings and platform behavior, Android backup or device transfer may also copy eligible app data; manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers according to Google's published retention policy. Clearing local app data does not necessarily remove previously transmitted diagnostic data or existing device backups.

Notifications

If you enable water reminders and grant notification permission, the app uses your chosen reminder time to show notifications. You may disable reminders in the app or manage notifications in device settings.

Security

The app uses Android's app-private storage for locally stored data. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Privacy policy website

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect and store visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version will be available through the app and its Google Play listing. We will update this policy before materially changing the handling of personal data or enabling additional services.
